## Decision

The previous `sources.md` and its outputs were ignored as research inputs. This run was independently seeded from current public first-party pages and canonical profiles. The register contains ten distinct expert/person brands and no more than three rows per expert.

- April Dunford, Peep Laja, Lenny Rachitsky, Rand Fishkin, Rob Walling, Jason Lemkin/SaaStr, and Dan Martell have first-party or canonical publisher evidence. Their claims remain opinions, surveys, predictions, or anecdotes according to the source type.

- Dave Gerhardt and Devin Reed have candidate LinkedIn rows because retrieval exposed profile/post discovery but not enough full text to support strong claims. They are not used for precise quantitative recommendations.
- SaaStr rows are labelled company/editorial voice; they are not silently converted into Jason Lemkin's personal statements.
- Interview host, guest, comments, reposts, and editorial summaries are kept separate. A page mentioning a person is not treated as that person's own recommendation unless the page identifies the speaker.

## Rejected or quarantined material

- The old Running Man YouTube transcript is irrelevant to B2B SaaS and is not used.
- The old unidentified YouTube videos are not used as expert evidence because channel/author identity was not proven.
- Any Dave Gerhardt or Devin Reed post whose canonical full text cannot be recovered remains candidate-only.
- A search snippet, engagement count, or company marketing claim is not used as independent validation of an outcome.

## Judge warnings

The result must not claim that these experts are universally correct or that a source-derived tactic will produce a particular business outcome. The validator should fail any recommendation that lacks a nearby public citation, has an unsupported number, or upgrades a candidate row to a factual claim.
